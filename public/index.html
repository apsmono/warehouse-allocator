How it decides

  1. Cold chain: a cold-chain product only ships from a warehouse with both refrigerated storage and refrigerated vans. Warehouses holding stock without a refrigerated fleet (such as W02 and W06) are excluded and reported.
  2. Cost: travel distance (km) plus a fixed 20 km overhead for each shipment (dispatch, paperwork, and hospital receiving check).
  3. Ranking: fill the most units first, then lowest total cost, then the earliest last arrival (shortest longest trip), then fewer shipments, then alphabetical warehouse ID.
  4. Split policy: when split shipments are allowed (default), an order may ship from several warehouses when that's the only way to fill it or when splitting is cheaper (each extra shipment adds 20 km). When off, a single warehouse ships what it can and any remaining units are reported as PARTIAL (back-ordered).

All distances are measured from the single customer location.
